Ms.Williams

Designed by a fan for other fans.

This typeface is lovingly named Ms.Williams (if ya nasty). I designed it as a fan, to empower and encourage other Hayley Williams’ fans to make their own Hayley Williams themed merch.

I built out a basic character set with numerals, punctuation marks, and two additional symbols I saw on hoodies, the cute devil and the H.W logo mark.

This set intentionally excludes curly braces so that you can type the devil and the H.W logo mark using curly braces on your keyboard or your phone keypad.

I am currently working on making this tool that will allow everyone to use their concert photos and use this font to turn them into a printable poster. Not everyone can afford Photoshop or learn complex design software. So, this is my way of making sure no one gets left behind. And you'll be able to do it on your phone.

It shows up as “Ms.Williams Shadow”. The pink and black are built in, so changing the text colour won't change them. Full colour in Illustrator, Photoshop, Mac apps, Windows 10/11 and browsers; apps without colour-font support, like Figma, show the letters without the outline and shadow.

Ms.Williams is free for personal, non-commercial use. You can use it for fan art, posters, stickers, scrapbooks, posts and gifts that you won't sell. This is a fan made project. As much as I'd love to be, I am not affiliated with Ms.Williams' marketing or design team. So, I do not get paid for this. I simply made it for the love of the game.
